How the key system works.

CIEL uses Luarmor for key verification. Here's exactly how to get set up.

01
Join the Discord
Head to our Discord server. All key purchases, support, and updates happen there. It's free to join.
02
Purchase a key
Pick your tier in the shop channel. Keys are delivered automatically after payment. You'll get a string like CIEL-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.
03
Open your executor
Launch Deepwoken, then open Wave, Xeno, or Solara. Paste the script into the execute tab.
04
Enter your key when prompted
The hub will open a key prompt on first run. Paste your key in, hit confirm. It'll be saved to your device after that.
05
You're in
Full hub loads instantly. All features unlocked based on your tier. Key works until your subscription expires.

Each key is device-locked

Keys are bound to your device on first use via Luarmor. You can't share them — one key, one machine. Need it reset? DM in Discord.

Keys expire with your plan

Day keys expire after 24h, week keys after 7 days, etc. Lifetime keys never expire. Renew anytime in Discord.

Patches & updates

When Deepwoken updates and breaks something, the script gets patched on the server side. Your key still works — you don't need to do anything.

No refunds on keys

All sales are final. Make sure you're using a supported executor before purchasing. Test with the free trial first if available.
