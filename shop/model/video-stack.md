## What partner material says about the method

Treat these as marketing. They are written by companies that profit from the stack.

- **Doba's blog on his method:** he favors "painkiller" products over "vitamins", meaning products that solve an immediate problem. Items hard to find in big-box stores that get an instant reaction in a social feed. Research by reading complaints on Reddit and Discord. Content is "short-form video ads that don't look like ads": AI-enhanced UGC, problem and solution demos, testimonials. A group of 10k-50k-follower micro-influencers rather than one big one.
- **AutoDS podcast page:** target low-barrier niches and upcoming events (Mother's Day, Father's Day). Check Google Trends to see if demand is lasting. AutoDS scans over 100,000 products a day and lists a marketplace of over 50 million. U.S. domestic suppliers offering two to five business days shipping.
- **A review of his course** says it focuses almost entirely on Facebook and Instagram ads. That differs from your organic-only plan.
