# Weekly review (Sundays, 45 minutes)

## 1. Fill one row in `kpi-sheet.csv`

Keep the real sheet in your own notes. This repo is public. The CSV here is the template.

## Definitions

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| posts | videos posted this week |
| total_views | views across all platforms |
| avg_3s_hold_pct | share of viewers still watching at 3 seconds (platform analytics) |
| link_clicks | clicks from bio or comments to the store |
| sessions | Shopify sessions from social |
| add_to_carts | Shopify add-to-cart events |
| checkouts_started | Shopify checkouts started |
| orders | paid orders |
| revenue | sum of order totals |
| doba_charges | sum of what Doba billed you (cost + shipping + card fee) |
| shopify_fees | card and gateway fees |
| refunds | money refunded |
| refund_pct | refunds divided by revenue, times 100 |
| support_tickets | messages that needed a reply |
| late_fulfilments | orders that missed the date you stated |
| net_after_fees | revenue minus doba_charges, shopify_fees and refunds. Before subscriptions. |
| float_open, float_close | cash you hold for Doba at the start and end of the week |
| notes | anything odd |

Derived: conversion = orders / sessions. Add-to-cart rate = add_to_carts / sessions. Checkout completion = orders / checkouts_started. Average order value = revenue / orders.

Targets from the plan: conversion 1-2% or better. These are targets, not promises.

## 2. Apply the kill rules (from the plan, my heuristics)

- [ ] Total outlay under $250 until 10 real sales. If not, stop and review.
- [ ] Any product with refunds or complaints past 8% of its orders: pause it.
- [ ] Any supplier with 3 or more missed or late fulfilments this week: pause its products.
- [ ] Day 40 with no add-to-carts: stop spending and review before any new money.
- [ ] No ads before a product has 3 or more videos over 5,000 views and 10 or more add-to-carts. (You are organic only; this is the gate for any later test.)
