# Logo prompts

Image generators draw symbols well and spell words badly. So: generate a **symbol**, then set the brand name beside it as live text in your theme's font.

## Quick placeholder (30 seconds)

Save as `logo.svg`, replace `[BRAND]`, upload to Shopify. Swap the colour for your palette's Ink. Replace it when you have a real mark.

```svg
<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="320" height="64" viewBox="0 0 320 64" role="img" aria-label="[BRAND]">
  <text x="0" y="46" font-family="Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif" font-size="44" font-weight="700" fill="#12343B">[BRAND]</text>
</svg>
```

## Prompts for the symbol

Use any image tool. Add the brand's two colours from `palette.md`.

1. `A simple flat vector symbol for a home-problem-solving brand, one shape, two colors (#12343B and #B5472F), centered on a plain white background, no text, no gradients, no shadows.`
2. `Minimal geometric mark: a rounded square with a small notch, flat vector, two colors, centered, white background, no text.`
3. `A friendly abstract leaf and roof silhouette combined into one shape, flat vector, single color #12343B, white background, no text.`
4. `A circle containing a simple check-mark curve, flat vector, two colors, white background, no text.`
5. `Monogram of the letter [LETTER] built from two overlapping rounded shapes, flat vector, two colors, white background.`
6. `A small house-shaped outline with a spark above it, flat line icon, uniform stroke, one color, white background, no text.`

Add to every prompt if the tool allows: `no watermark, no existing brand logos, not a copy of any known logo`.

## Test before you use it

1. Shrink it to 32 pixels. Is it still readable?
2. Place it on a light background and a dark one.
3. Run a reverse image search on the mark to check it does not resemble a known logo.
4. Run the brand name plus the mark past the trademark search in `names.md`.
5. Keep the file in two forms: the symbol alone, and symbol plus name.

## Do not

- Do not copy a competitor's mark, a swoosh, a bitten fruit, a famous animal silhouette or anything close.
- Do not use text rendered by the image tool inside the logo.
