# Palette

Three options. Pick one and use it everywhere. Contrast ratios below were computed with the WCAG formula. Normal text needs 4.5:1 or better, and every pair here clears it.

| Role | A Calm | B Clean | C Warm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink (text, headings) | `#12343B` | `#1B2A41` | `#2B2D42` |
| Paper (page background) | `#F7F3EC` | `#F5F7FA` | `#FFF8F0` |
| Accent (buttons, links) | `#B5472F` | `#0B6E75` | `#A9432D` |
| Soft (cards, bands) | `#D9E6E3` | `#E4EAF2` | `#F2E3D5` |
| Muted (secondary text) | `#4A5A5E` | `#51607A` | `#5C5F73` |

| Pair | A Calm | B Clean | C Warm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink on Paper | 12.02 | 13.46 | 12.81 |
| Ink on Soft | 10.37 | 11.93 | 10.74 |
| Muted on Paper | 6.51 | 5.92 | 5.97 |
| Muted on Soft | 5.62 | 5.25 | 5.01 |
| White text on Accent (buttons) | 5.37 | 6.00 | 5.95 |
| Accent on Paper (links) | 4.86 | 5.59 | 5.65 |

## Use

- Page background: Paper. Cards and bands: Soft. Text: Ink. Secondary text: Muted.
- Buttons: Accent background, white text. One button style only.
- Links: Accent, underlined.
- Keep the same palette in the store, the videos' on-screen text and the logo.
- Fonts: start with the theme's default heading and body fonts. If you change them, use one sans-serif for body and at most one other for headings.

## Check

Re-run the contrast math if you change any hex value. Do not use Accent as text on Soft: that pair was not measured.
