# Brand names

You choose the niche on Day 3, so choose the name after. Until then these are raw ideas for an umbrella brand that works across several product types.

**None has been checked** for trademarks, domains or social handles. The `.store` registry lookup refused this server's requests, so domain availability is unknown. Common words are usually taken.

## Rules for a good name

1. Two or three syllables, easy to say and spell on first hearing.
2. Works across product types, so adding a product never forces a rename.
3. No health or cure words: relief, cure, heal, therapy, clinical. They invite claims you cannot support.
4. No existing trademark in your product area. Search the free USPTO trademark database.
5. The `.store` domain and the same handle on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube are free.
6. Nothing that means something rude or odd in another language.
7. Not a famous brand with one letter changed.

## Prompt for Claude once the niche is chosen

> Niche: [NICHE]. Customer: [WHO]. Products: [3 PRODUCTS]. Give me 30 brand names, 2-3 syllables, no health claims, each with a one-line reason and the likely meaning in Spanish, French and German. Avoid anything that sounds like an existing brand in [AREA].

## Checks before you buy (15 minutes)

1. USPTO trademark search for the name and close spellings, in the retail class and in the class of your product type.
2. Your target state's business registry, and a plain web search: is a store already using this name?
3. The `.store` domain at the registrar. About $0.99 for year one with the video's code. // FILL: the renewal price before you buy.
4. Handles on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Same name everywhere.
5. Say the name aloud to someone. Can they spell it back?
