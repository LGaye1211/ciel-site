# Brand voice

Plain, calm, problem-first. The store talks like a sensible friend who found something that works and says so without hype.

## Rules

1. Start with the problem in the customer's own words, not with the product name.
2. Short sentences. Active verbs. One idea per sentence.
3. U.S. spelling. No slang that only part of the country uses.
4. Say what the product does, using the supplier's specifications. Say nothing the listing does not support.
5. Numbers only when they are true and sourced: dimensions, capacity, material, warranty.
6. Be honest about delivery and returns. State them in plain numbers.
7. One call to action per page or post.

## Words to use

simple, easy, designed to, made for, helps with, fits, holds, folds, lasts (only with a spec), ships from a U.S. warehouse, free U.S. shipping (if true)

## Words to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| guaranteed, miracle, instantly, cures, heals, treats | claims the listing cannot back, and in some categories regulated |
| clinically proven, doctor recommended, dermatologist tested | needs evidence you do not have |
| best, #1, top-rated, award-winning | unsupported superlatives |
| limited stock, only N left, selling fast | untrue unless it is the real stock count |
| thousands of happy customers, as seen on | untrue for a new store |
| "I tried it", "I use it every day", "my dog loves it" | a fake testimonial when the speaker never used it |
| before and after photos | untrue unless real customer photos with permission |

## Good and bad

**Bad:** "The #1 miracle bed that cures dog anxiety! Only 3 left!"
**Good:** "A raised-edge bed for dogs that like to curl up. Machine-washable cover. Free U.S. shipping."

**Bad:** "I've been using this for months and it changed my life."
**Good:** "Here is what it does: [one listed function]."

## Tone check before posting

1. Would a skeptical stranger believe every sentence?
2. Is every claim in the claims log with a source (`../content/claims-checklist.md`)?
3. Does it read like a person wrote it, not a template?
