# Welcome flow (3 emails)

Trigger: a new subscriber who ticked an unticked opt-in box. Every email carries your real postal address, a working unsubscribe link and an honest subject line (U.S. email law, CAN-SPAM; verify with a professional). Replace every `[BRACKET]` inside your email app, not in this repo.

## Email 1 - immediately

**Subject:** Welcome to [BRAND]
**Preview:** What we do and what to expect

```
Hi [FIRST NAME],

Thanks for signing up. [BRAND] sells [PRODUCT AREA] that help with [PROBLEM].
We only list products we can describe honestly.

What to expect: a short email now and then. You can unsubscribe in one click.

[ONLY IF YOU OFFER ONE:] Here is [CODE] for [X]% off your first order. It works until [DATE].

Take a look: [STORE LINK]
Questions? Reply to this email.

[BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]
Unsubscribe: [UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]
```

Rule: only offer a code you will honor. No countdown timers.

## Email 2 - day 2

**Subject:** About [PROBLEM]
**Preview:** One simple idea

```
Hi [FIRST NAME],

[PROBLEM] shows up for a lot of people. They tell us [CUSTOMER PHRASE].

[PRODUCT] is made to [LISTED FUNCTION]. It [ONE LISTED SPEC].

See how it works: [PRODUCT LINK]

[BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]
Unsubscribe: [UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]
```

Rule: no testimonials, no "customers love it", no results claims.

## Email 3 - day 5

**Subject:** Shipping, returns and how to reach us
**Preview:** The practical details

```
Hi [FIRST NAME],

The practical details:
- Orders ship within [X] business days from a U.S. warehouse and typically arrive in [Y]-[Z] business days after shipping.
- Returns: [X] days from delivery. Details: [RETURNS LINK]
- Questions: reply to this email. We answer within [N] business hours.

[STORE LINK]

[BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]
Unsubscribe: [UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]
```
