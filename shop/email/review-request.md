# Review request

Send [7-10] days after tracking shows delivery. One email, no reminders.

**Subject:** How is your [PRODUCT]?
**Preview:** A short, honest review helps other shoppers

```
Hi [FIRST NAME],

It has been a little while since your [PRODUCT] arrived. If you have a minute, an honest review helps other shoppers decide. Good or bad, we want to hear it.

Leave a review: [REVIEW LINK]

If something is wrong, reply to this email first and we will fix it.

[BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]
Unsubscribe: [UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]
```

## Rules (U.S. FTC rule on reviews, verify with a professional)

1. Ask every buyer, not only the happy ones. Do not screen who gets the request.
2. Do not offer a reward, discount or refund in exchange for a positive review. Do not condition anything on rating.
3. Publish negative reviews that follow your content rules. Do not suppress them.
4. Never write, buy or import reviews. Never ask friends or family to review a product they did not buy and use.
5. Do not edit a customer's words.
6. If you show review counts or stars, they come from real verified buyers only.
7. A reply to a negative review should offer a fix, not argue.
