# About page (template)

> Template. Not published as is. Do not invent a founder story, a workshop, a team, awards, years in business or a customer count. Everything on this page must be true on the day it goes live.

## Copy

**About [BRAND]**

[BRAND] sells [PRODUCT AREA] for people who [the problem, plainly]. We look for products that solve one everyday problem well, and we pass on the ones that do not.

**How it works**

We do not keep products in our own warehouse. Orders go to U.S. suppliers, and your order ships from a U.S. warehouse with tracking. Delivery is typically [Y]-[Z] business days after shipping.

**Who runs this**

[BRAND] is run by [BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS]. Write to us at [EMAIL]. We read every message.

**What we promise**

- We describe products only as the supplier's specifications describe them.
- If something arrives damaged, defective or not as described, we make it right under our Returns policy.
- We never write fake reviews.

## Notes for you

- If the business is run from outside the U.S., say so. A true line is better than an implied U.S. address. "We are run from [COUNTRY] and ship from U.S. warehouses" is fine.
- Do not call the brand "family-owned", "handmade" or "designed by us" unless it is.
- If you add a photo, use your own. Do not use stock photos of "the team".
