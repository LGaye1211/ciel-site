# Home page (template)

> Template. Replace every `[BRACKET]`. Write only what the supplier listing supports. No fake reviews, counters or badges.

## Blocks, in order

**1. Hero**
- Headline (the problem, not the product): `[The one problem, in the customer's words]`
- Sub-line: `[BRAND] [what it does in one plain sentence].`
- Button: `Shop [PRODUCT]`
- Image: the supplier photo that matches the product exactly.

**2. The problem**
Two or three short lines in the words people use when they complain (from the Reddit and Discord mining): `[phrase 1]`, `[phrase 2]`.

**3. The product**
- Name, price, one-line benefit, `Free U.S. shipping`.
- Three bullet points taken straight from the listing's specs: `[spec 1]`, `[spec 2]`, `[spec 3]`.
- Button: `Add to cart`.

**4. How it works**
Three steps, each one line, each true: `1. [Step]  2. [Step]  3. [Step]`.

**5. Delivery and returns (honest)**
- `Ships from a U.S. warehouse. Typically arrives in [Y]-[Z] business days after shipping.`
- `[X]-day returns. Details in our Returns policy.`
- `Questions? [EMAIL]. We reply within [N] business hours.`

**6. FAQ**
Three questions from `faq.md`.

**7. Footer**
Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, Contact, `[BUSINESS NAME]`, `[ADDRESS]`.

## Never include

Countdown timers. "Only N left" unless that is the real stock count. "Sold N in 24 hours". "As seen on". "Thousands of happy customers". Star ratings without real reviews. Before and after images that are not real. Medical or "clinically proven" claims.
