# Returns and refunds policy (template)

> Template, not legal advice. Have a professional review it before launch. **Do not promise more than the supplier's return terms unless you accept the cost.** The margin gate assumes a 15% refund and loss allowance. A generous policy raises that number.

**Return window**
You may return an item within [X] days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging.

**Damaged, defective or wrong item**
Write to [EMAIL] within [N] days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. We will send a replacement or refund you. For items under $[AMOUNT] we may not ask you to send the item back.

**Changed your mind**
Write to [EMAIL] for return instructions. Return shipping is paid by [the customer / us]. [A restocking fee of $X may apply.] // FILL: match the supplier's terms.

**How to start a return**
1. Write to [EMAIL] with your order number and the reason.
2. We reply with the return address and steps. Do not send anything back before you hear from us. The return address is a U.S. address and may differ from where the order shipped.
3. Ship it with tracking and keep the receipt.

**Refunds**
Refunds go to the original payment method within [5-10] business days after we receive and inspect the return, or after we approve a refund without return. Your bank may take extra days to show it.

**Cancellations**
You may cancel before the order ships. Write to [EMAIL] as soon as you can. After it ships, use the return process.

**Items we cannot take back**
[List: opened hygiene items, custom items, clearance items marked final sale]. // FILL: only list what the supplier also refuses.

**Late orders**
If we cannot ship by the date we stated, we will tell you and offer a full refund if you want to cancel.

**Contact**
[BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS], [EMAIL]

## Notes for you

- Before launch, read the supplier's return policy for every product on the shortlist. Write the narrowest promise that covers all of them.
- The return address must be a real U.S. address that accepts returns. If a supplier will not take returns, do not promise returns on that product. Offer replacement or refund for damage only.
- Keep a record of every return decision. You will need it for disputes (`../../ops/refund-chargeback-sop.md`).
