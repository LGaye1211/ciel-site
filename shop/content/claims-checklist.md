# Claims checklist and claims log

Every video, page and email passes this list. It exists because you never see the product and the audience is U.S. consumers.

## Per-video checklist

- [ ] Every statement in the voice-over and on-screen text appears in the claims log for this product.
- [ ] No person in the video says or implies they used, bought, reviewed or recommend the product.
- [ ] No before-and-after image unless it is a real customer's, with written permission.
- [ ] No health, safety, performance or comparison claim the supplier listing does not make.
- [ ] No fake scarcity ("only N left", "selling fast") or fake popularity ("thousands sold").
- [ ] The product in the video matches the supplier photos: shape, color, label, proportions, size.
- [ ] Realistic AI people or scenes carry the platform's AI label where it exists.
- [ ] No watermark from another app, and the clip is your own edit, not a reupload.
- [ ] The caption states the real shipping and returns terms or leaves them out.
- [ ] No other brand's logo, music you do not have rights to, or celebrity likeness.
- [ ] The link goes to the product page that matches the video.

## Platform and legal notes (verify; sources are blogs and summaries)

- **TikTok:** reduces distribution for reposted, watermarked or unoriginal video, and its guidelines list unoriginal content and IP rights as a rule. No official statement covers supplier footage, so use it only with written permission. Realistic-looking AI people or scenes are expected to carry a label (third-party sources). Re-check TikTok's Community Guidelines before posting.
- **Instagram and YouTube:** AI-disclosure rules were **not researched**. // FILL: read each platform's current policy before posting there.
- **FTC (U.S.):** the 2024 rule bans fake or false reviews and testimonials, including AI-generated ones by people who do not exist, with civil penalties reported up to $52,000 per violation. AI UGC tools often default to "I tried this for weeks" scripts. This kit's scripts use a narrator and never claim personal use. Your buyers are U.S. consumers, so treat this as binding. Not legal advice.
- **Regulated goods:** no supplements, cosmetics, kids' products, battery or mains electronics, or branded goods.

```
functions the listing supports:    [list]

```

## Review habit

Re-check the listing when you re-check the price. If the supplier changes the listing, the log changes first, then the videos.
