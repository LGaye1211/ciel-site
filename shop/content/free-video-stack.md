# Free video stack ($0)

Goal: one honest 10-15 second clip a day for $0, without misrepresenting the product. Two routes. Use A for safety and B for hero shots. The paid upgrade is `ai-video-tool.md`.

Everything about free-tier rights below is **secondhand and unverified**. It comes from blog posts, not official terms pages.

## Route A: photo-and-text video (always $0, safest)

Built from the supplier's own photos, so the product cannot warp or change.

Tools: CapCut free, on phone or desktop.

1. **Photos.** Take 4-6 photos from the Doba listing: different angles and any use shots. Do not use another seller's photos. Crop out any supplier logo, watermark or text that is not on the product.
2. **Structure.** Follow `scripts.md`: hook text on photo 1 (0-2 s), the problem line (2-6 s), the listed feature on photos 2-4 (6-11 s), one listing spec (11-14 s), call to action (14-15 s).
3. **Motion.** Add a slow zoom or pan to each photo with CapCut's keyframes or animation tools. One photo per 2-3 seconds.
4. **Voice.** Record the narration on your phone in your own voice. Your own recording has the clearest rights. The narrator rule applies: no "I used this".
5. **Captions.** Use the auto-captions, then fix the spelling.
6. **Music.** None is safest. If you want music, use a track with a clear commercial license. TikTok business accounts are generally limited to commercial-licensed sounds. // FILL: check TikTok's current rule for your account type before posting.
7. **Export** at 1080 x 1920 with no watermark. Upload natively to each platform.

CapCut licensing, as one secondary source reads it: rights are asset by asset. Items flagged for commercial use are fine. A project that mixes in a non-commercial item becomes non-commercial. Built-in music is the main risk. So use only items marked for commercial use, or your own recordings and photos. // FILL: read CapCut's current Materials License before the first post.

## Route B: free AI-video credits for hero clips

Reported free tiers (secondhand, Oct 2026):

| Tool | Reported free tier | Commercial use |
|---|---|---|
| Seedance 2 | about 100 refreshing daily credits | reported in many regions. Confirm yours. |
| Kling | about 66 daily credits | sources disagree |
| Hailuo | about 10-20 daily credits | one source says allowed |
| Pika | about 150 credits a month | one source says allowed |
| Luma | about 30 generations a month, no watermark on standard output | not stated |
| Runway | free tier exports clean | reportedly **not** for commercial use |
| Veo | no free tier found | n/a |

Before the first commercial post, open each tool's official terms and pricing page and confirm:
1. Commercial use is allowed on the free tier.
2. Watermark rules.
3. Any region restriction.
4. Any rule about uploading product photos as input.

Take a dated screenshot of each page and keep it in your own notes.

Clip recipe and fidelity check: same as `ai-video-tool.md`. Use at least 3 reference photos, keep hands out of frame, and score every clip on the five points before posting. A clip that changes the label, logo, shape, color or size does not get posted.

## Mixing the two

- Use Route A for most days. It is faster to fix and cannot misrepresent.
- Use a Route B clip for the opening or the reveal when it passes the fidelity check.
- Re-cuts (`calendar-30-day.md`) are Route A edits of an earlier clip with a new first 3 seconds and a new caption.

## Daily time

First week: about 45-60 minutes per clip. Once you reuse your CapCut project as a template: about 20-30 minutes.

## When to pay

Move to EcomBos Pro (`ai-video-tool.md`) when any one is true:
- the free tools fail the fidelity test on 2 of 3 products
- you cannot make a clip a day
- a product reaches 10 add-to-carts

## Do not

- Do not post a clip the fidelity check failed.
- Do not use built-in CapCut music, templates or effects that lack a commercial mark.
- Do not let any person or AI avatar say they used the product.
- Do not reuse a clip another seller already posted.
- Do not skip the AI label where the platform asks for it (`claims-checklist.md`).
