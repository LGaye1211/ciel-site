# 50 hooks

Templates for the first 2 seconds. Replace `[BRACKETS]` with the customer's own words from your complaint mining. No hook claims personal use, results or a purchase. Each ID is referenced from the calendar.

## Problem first (H-01 to H-10)

- H-01: If [PROBLEM] keeps happening, watch this.
- H-02: [PROBLEM]? There is a simple fix.
- H-03: Tired of [PROBLEM] every [TIME]?
- H-04: [PROBLEM] is more common than you think.
- H-05: This is why [PROBLEM] keeps coming back.
- H-06: Still [OLD WAY]? There is an easier way.
- H-07: Nobody warns you about [PROBLEM].
- H-08: If your [THING] keeps [ANNOYING THING], read this.
- H-09: Here is what to do when [PROBLEM] happens.
- H-10: The [PROBLEM] fix that takes under a minute. (Only if the listing supports the time.)

## Questions (H-11 to H-18)

- H-11: Why does [THING] always [PROBLEM]?
- H-12: What if [PROBLEM] had a simple answer?
- H-13: Ever notice how [ANNOYING THING] never gets fixed?
- H-14: Can [THING] really [LISTED FUNCTION]?
- H-15: What do you do when [PROBLEM] shows up at [TIME]?
- H-16: Is your [THING] making [PROBLEM] worse?
- H-17: How do people deal with [PROBLEM]?
- H-18: What is the one thing that stops [PROBLEM]?

## Mistakes and myths (H-19 to H-26)

- H-19: Stop doing this if you want to avoid [PROBLEM].
- H-20: The common mistake that causes [PROBLEM].
- H-21: You do not need [EXPENSIVE THING] for [PROBLEM].
- H-22: Three things that do not fix [PROBLEM], and one that might.
- H-23: [OLD WAY] is not the answer to [PROBLEM].
- H-24: What most people get wrong about [PROBLEM].
- H-25: If [OLD WAY] has not worked, try this instead.
- H-26: The [PROBLEM] myth that will not go away.

## Demo and reveal (H-27 to H-34)

- H-27: Watch what this does.
- H-28: Here is how it works in ten seconds.
- H-29: [THING] that [LISTED FUNCTION]. Look.
- H-30: One piece, one job: [LISTED FUNCTION].
- H-31: Set up in [LISTED STEPS]. That is it. (Only if the listing says so.)
- H-32: Look at the [LISTED FEATURE].
- H-33: This is the part that matters: [LISTED FEATURE].

## Comparison (H-35 to H-40)

- H-35: [OLD WAY] vs [PRODUCT]. Same job, different result.
- H-36: Why [PRODUCT] instead of [BIG-BOX ITEM].
- H-37: What you get with [PRODUCT] that [ALTERNATIVE] leaves out.
- H-38: [ALTERNATIVE] costs more. Here is what is different.
- H-39: Two ways to handle [PROBLEM]. Compare.
- H-40: Not every [CATEGORY] is the same. Look at the [FEATURE].

## Use case and gifts (H-41 to H-46)

- H-41: For anyone who [AUDIENCE TRAIT].
- H-42: A useful gift for someone who [AUDIENCE TRAIT].
- H-43: If you live with [SITUATION], this is for you.
- H-44: Small apartment? Look at this.
- H-45: Good for [USE 1], [USE 2] and [USE 3]. (Only listed uses.)
- H-46: Something practical for [OCCASION].

## Direct (H-47 to H-50)

- H-47: [PRODUCT]. Free U.S. shipping. Link in bio. (Only if true.)
- H-48: This is [PRODUCT]. Here is what it does.
- H-49: Meet [PRODUCT].
- H-50: Comment LINK and we will send it to you. (Only if the comment automation is set up.)

## Rules

1. First 2 seconds carry the hook as on-screen text and voice-over.
2. Use the customer's exact phrases from `../research/autods-playbook.md` Prompt 1.
3. Never turn a hook into a first-person claim. "I tried" and "I use" are banned.
4. Test two hooks per clip idea. Keep the one with better 3-second hold.
