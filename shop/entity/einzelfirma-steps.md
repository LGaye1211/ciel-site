# Swiss sole proprietorship (Einzelfirma): the launch lane

Not legal or tax advice. The sources are Swiss accounting and company-formation blogs. Everything here is **unverified** against an official source. Confirm each step with your canton's tax office and AHV compensation office (Ausgleichskasse).

## What the sources say

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Set-up cost | under CHF 500, no share capital, no notary |
| Commercial register | not required below CHF 100,000 turnover; required above it (one-off fee about CHF 120 in one source) |
| VAT (MWST) | registration required at CHF 100,000 worldwide taxable turnover; standard rate 8.1% |
| GmbH instead | CHF 20,000 capital fully paid in, plus CHF 800-9,200 setup (sources vary). Not for now. |
| Liability | you are personally liable for the business |

## Steps

1. **Check your employment contract.** If you are employed, a side business may need your employer's consent. // FILL
2. **Register as self-employed with the AHV compensation office** of your canton, so social-insurance contributions are set on your business income. Ask when registration is required and what the minimum income threshold is. // FILL
3. **Tell your tax office** that you have self-employment income. Ask how to report income and expenses and which forms apply. // FILL
4. **Bank.** Keep business money apart from private money. A separate account is simplest. A sole proprietorship can use a private account, but a separate one makes the books and the yearly return easier.
5. **Bookkeeping.** Record every sale, every Doba charge, every subscription and every fee. Keep invoices and receipts. The KPI sheet in `../ops/kpi-sheet.csv` is not accounting.
6. **VAT watch.** Track worldwide taxable turnover. Ask the adviser whether sales made outside Switzerland count toward the CHF 100,000 line, and how U.S. sales fulfilled from U.S. warehouses are treated. // FILL
7. **Insurance.** Ask about accident cover for the self-employed, health cover and business liability cover. // FILL
8. **Business name.** A sole proprietorship can trade under a name. Ask whether the name must be registered. // FILL
9. **Declare the income.** Worldwide income is taxed in Switzerland, including profit from sales to U.S. customers. Ask how U.S. taxes paid, if any, are credited.

## What this lane does not give you

- No U.S. entity, EIN, U.S. address or U.S. bank account.
- Possible friction opening U.S. payment accounts or getting a resale certificate. See `us-llc-runbook.md`.
- No liability shield.

## When to move to the LLC lane

When Doba or a supplier requires a resale certificate, when a payment processor refuses you, or when revenue passes the point where the extra yearly cost is worth the benefit. Decide with the adviser, not on a deadline.
