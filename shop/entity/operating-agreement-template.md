# Single-member LLC operating agreement (template)

> Template only, not legal advice. A U.S. lawyer must review it for your state before you sign. Replace every `[BRACKET]`. Keep the signed copy in your own records, never in this public repo.

**OPERATING AGREEMENT OF [LLC NAME], LLC**
A [STATE] limited liability company
Effective date: [DATE]

This Operating Agreement is made by [MEMBER NAME], the sole member ("Member"), for [LLC NAME], LLC (the "Company").

## 1. Formation
The Company was formed by filing Articles of Organization with the [STATE] Secretary of State on [DATE]. The Member adopts this Agreement as the Company's operating agreement.

## 2. Name, office and agent
The Company's name is [LLC NAME], LLC. Its registered office and agent in [STATE] are [REGISTERED AGENT NAME AND ADDRESS]. Its principal place of business is [ADDRESS].

## 3. Purpose
The Company may engage in any lawful business. Its initial business is operating an online retail store selling products fulfilled by third-party suppliers.

## 4. Member and capital
The Member is [MEMBER NAME]. The Member's initial capital contribution is $[AMOUNT], paid on [DATE]. The Member may contribute more at any time. Contributions are recorded in the Company's books and are not loans unless written as loans.

## 5. Management
The Company is managed by the Member. The Member may sign contracts, open accounts and take any action for the Company. The Member may appoint officers or agents in writing.

## 6. Distributions
The Company distributes cash to the Member when the Member decides, but not if the Company would be unable to pay its debts as they fall due.

## 7. Tax treatment
For U.S. federal income tax purposes the Company is intended to be disregarded as an entity separate from its owner unless the Member elects otherwise. The Member acknowledges that a foreign-owned disregarded Company may have to file annual information returns. The Member will keep records that support these filings. // FILL: the CPA confirms.

## 8. Books, records and bank accounts
The Company keeps its own books. It holds money only in accounts opened in its own name. The Member will not pay Company expenses from personal accounts, or personal expenses from Company accounts, except by documented transfer recorded as a contribution, loan or distribution.

## 9. Limited liability
The Member is not personally liable for the Company's debts or obligations solely because they are the Member. The Member acknowledges this protection can be lost if the Company's and the Member's affairs are not kept separate.

## 10. Transfer
The Member may transfer all or part of the membership interest. A transferee becomes a member only if admitted in writing.

## 11. Dissolution
The Company dissolves on the Member's written decision, on the Member's death or incapacity unless the successor continues it as the law allows, or when a court orders it. After dissolution the Company pays its debts, then distributes what remains to the Member or successors.

## 12. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE].

## 13. Amendments
Only the Member may amend this Agreement, in writing.

## 14. Signature

Member: ______________________   Name: [MEMBER NAME]   Date: [DATE]

## Notes for the lawyer

- Foreign-owned single-member LLC with the member resident in Switzerland.
- Ask about succession provisions, indemnification of the Member, and the effect on liability protection of managing the Company from outside the U.S.
- Confirm whether the state requires any additional provisions.
