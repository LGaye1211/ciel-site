# Questions for the CPA and the Swiss adviser

Book one call with a U.S. CPA who works with foreign owners, and one with a Swiss tax adviser (Treuhaender or Steuerberater) who knows U.S. LLCs. Send the facts first. Write down the answers in your own notes.

## Facts to send (keep them out of this repo)

- You are a Swiss tax resident, a sole owner, running an online store from Switzerland.
- Customers are in the United States only. Goods ship from U.S. supplier warehouses. You never hold stock.
- The U.S. platform is Doba (U.S. suppliers), the store is on Shopify, fees are paid by card.
- Expected first-year revenue: [RANGE]. Expected net profit: [RANGE].
- Considering a Wyoming or New Mexico LLC. Alternatively a Swiss sole proprietorship.

## U.S. CPA

1. Does selling U.S. inventory to U.S. customers through a U.S. LLC create income effectively connected with a U.S. trade or business? Do I file Form 1040-NR, and what is taxed?
2. Which state should I form in, and which state sales-tax permits do I need? Where does nexus arise for a store whose suppliers ship from several states?
3. Do I need a resale certificate to order from Doba suppliers? Which state issues it? Does it require an SSN or ITIN?
4. Form 5472 and the pro forma Form 1120: who prepares it, what does it cost each year, and does any year qualify for the no-transaction exception?
5. How should I fund the LLC and take money out so the reportable transactions are clean?
6. Which forms do platforms and processors need from me (W-8BEN-E, W-8BEN, others), and what is reported on 1099-K?
7. Treaty benefits between the U.S. and Switzerland that apply to this income. How do I claim them?
8. Sales tax: when must I start collecting in each state, and what does Shopify need configured?
9. What do you charge for the formation-year filings and for each later year?

## Swiss adviser

1. How does Switzerland classify a U.S. LLC owned by a Swiss resident: as a partnership or as a corporation? The Federal Court has gone one way in a case, while the statute points the other way. Which applies in my canton, and does it change with where the LLC is managed?
2. If it is a corporation: how are its value (wealth tax) and my distributions taxed? One source says distributions are taxed on 50-70% at a 10% or higher holding.
3. If the LLC is managed from Switzerland, could it be treated as resident in Switzerland for tax purposes?
4. How do I declare profit from a Swiss sole proprietorship that sells to U.S. customers? How are U.S. taxes credited under the treaty?
5. Do sales made outside Switzerland count toward the CHF 100,000 VAT threshold? Am I liable to register for VAT?
6. AHV contributions on this income: when, how much, who registers me?
7. Does my employer need to consent to this business?
8. Insurance I should have.
9. Does running both the sole proprietorship and an LLC make sense, or one?
